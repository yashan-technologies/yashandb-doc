In this chapter and all product documentation, when describing quantities:

- K represents 1024
- M represents 1024*1024
- G represents 1024*1024*1024

When describing lengths:

- KB represents 1024 bytes
- MB represents 1024*1024 bytes
- GB represents 1024*1024*1024 bytes
