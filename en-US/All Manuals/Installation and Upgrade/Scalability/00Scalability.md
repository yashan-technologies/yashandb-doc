YashanDB supports dynamic scaling, allowing linear expansion and resource adjustment of database service capabilities without affecting usage.

- [Standalone primary/standby scaling](Scalability of Standalone Deployment/00Scalability of Standalone Deployment): Scaling for the standby database and cascade standby database in Standalone Deployment.

- [YAC scaling](Scalability of YAC Deployment/00Scalability of YAC Deployment): Online scaling of instances within the cluster in YAC Deployment.

- [ISC distributed cluster scaling](Scalability of ISC Distributed Cluster Deployment/00Scalability of ISC Distributed Cluster Deployment): Allows scaling for CN nodes, DN groups, and standby nodes within DN or MN groups.
