The high availability of YashanDB is reflected in two aspects. On one hand, it has the ability to restore from backup sets after database data corruption. On the other hand, in a multi-node architecture, the system has the capability of failover and recovery when any node fails.

- [Backup and Recovery](./备份恢复) describes the backup and recovery capabilities of YashanDB.

- [Flashback](./闪回) introduces the capabilities of full database flashback, flashback table recovery, and flashback queries in YashanDB.

- [Replication and Switching](./主备复制及切换) describes the primary/standby architecture of YashanDB based on redo replication.

- [Automatic Election](./自动选主) describes the leader election capabilities in the primary/standby architecture and YAC architecture of YashanDB.
