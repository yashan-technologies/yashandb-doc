In Standalone Deployment, users can perform physical standby database scaling configuration on a running environment as needed:

- [Standbys Scaling](./Standbys Scaling): Scaling out or scaling in physical standby databases for the existing environment.

- [Cascading Standbys Scaling](./Cascading Standbys Scaling): Scaling out or scaling in cascade standby databases to a specific physical standby database.

- [Standbys within Dual Rep-Group Scaling](./Standbys within Dual Rep-Group Scaling): Scaling out or scaling in standby databases in the primary replication group and non-primary nodes in the standby replication group (i.e., cascade standbys) in Dual Rep-Group Primary-Standby Deployment.
