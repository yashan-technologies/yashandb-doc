The security requirements for the system refer to the establishment of security protection mechanisms and the implementation of security management measures to protect computer hardware, software, and data from accidental and malicious damage, alteration, or leakage.

Security requirements are typically divided into confidentiality, integrity, and availability (CIA):

- Confidentiality: Data must not be disclosed to unauthorized users or entities. This is often achieved through encryption, access controls, and other means to meet confidentiality requirements.

- Integrity: Data must remain unmodified, undamaged, and unreleased in unauthorized circumstances during storage and transmission. This is typically achieved through access controls and integrity checks to meet integrity requirements.

- Availability: Data must be correctly accessible by authorized users and entities and must be able to be restored under abnormal conditions. This is usually achieved through backups, redundancy, firewalls, and other measures to meet availability requirements.
