## 23.5.2.100

The following lists the major and notable changes in v23.5.2.100 of YashanDB compared to v23.5.1.100. If you discover any changes during your use that are not listed in this document but impact your company's business, please consult our technical support for detailed explanations.

### Upgrade Notes

The following table shows the range of old versions that support offline upgrades to the current version.

|Deployment Type       |Old Version Range                             |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Standalone Deployment              | 22.2.16 and earlier<br/>23.1.5 and earlier<br/>23.2.14 and earlier<br/>23.3.3 and earlier<br/>23.4.6 and earlier |
| YAC Deployment                     | 23.1.5 and earlier<br/>23.2.14 and earlier<br/>23.3.3 and earlier<br/>23.4.6 and earlier |
| ISC Distributed Cluster Deployment | 23.1.5 and earlier<br/>23.2.14 and earlier<br/>23.3.3 and earlier<br/>23.4.6 and earlier |

### Installation and Upgrade

|Change Item |Change |Content                                             |
