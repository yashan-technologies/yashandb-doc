## 23.4.9.100

The following lists the major and notable changes in version 23.4.9.100 of YashanDB compared to version 23.4.8.100. If you discover any changes during your use that are not listed in this document but impact your company's business, please consult our technical support for detailed explanations.

| YAC Deployment                     | 23.1.5 and earlier<br/>23.2.13 and earlier<br/>23.3.3 and earlier<br/>23.4.8 and earlier |
| ISC Distributed Cluster Deployment | 23.1.5 and earlier<br/>23.2.12 and earlier<br/>23.3.3 and earlier<br/>23.4.8 and earlier |
